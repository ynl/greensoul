GreenSoul Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 8, 2024

Welcome to GreenSoul! We are committed to protecting your privacy and personal information security. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Email Address: For account registration, login authentication, and communication

1.2 User-Generated Content

  • Plant Photos: Images you upload for plant identification
  • Chat Messages: Conversations with your plant companion
  • Plant Information: Plant name, personality traits, species, etc.

1.3 Device and Usage Data

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version
  • Language Preference: Your chosen app interface language
🔒 Important: We do NOT collect sensitive information such as location or contacts

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Core Services: Plant identification, AI conversations, cross-device data sync
  • Personalization: Create unique plant personalities based on species
  • Improvement: Optimize app performance and fix issues
  • Account Management: User authentication, password recovery
✅ Our Commitment: We will NEVER sell your personal information to third parties or use it for advertising

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Storage Location

  • Cloud Storage: Encrypted storage via Supabase (hosted on AWS)
  • Local Storage: Some data cached on your device for performance

3.2 Security Measures

  • ✅ All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption (TLS 1.2+)
  • ✅ Passwords stored using industry-standard encryption
  • ✅ Strict access control
  • ✅ Regular security audits

4. Third-Party Services

To provide core functionality, we use the following trusted third-party services:

  • AI Services (OpenRouter): Process plant identification and conversation generation
  • Cloud Infrastructure (Supabase/AWS): Provide secure data storage

We do NOT share data with:

  • ❌ Advertising networks
  • ❌ Marketing companies
  • ❌ Data brokers

5. Your Privacy Rights

  • Access: View your personal information in the app at any time
  • Correction: Update or modify inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Permanently delete all data through "Delete Account"
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time
📮 Exercise Rights: Contact us at yilin01@icloud.com

6. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the Profile page. Upon deletion:

  • ✅ Your account information
  • ✅ All plant companion data
  • ✅ All chat history
  • ✅ All uploaded photos
⚠️ Important: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Deleted data will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at yilin01@icloud.com.

8. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active
  • Chat History: Retained permanently until you delete or close your account
  • Usage Logs: Automatically deleted after 90 days
  • After Deletion: All data permanently removed within 30 days

9. International Data Transfer

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your country of residence. We ensure:

  • ✅ Use of globally compliant cloud service providers (AWS)
  • ✅ Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • ✅ Third-party providers comply with GDPR and other privacy regulations

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When we make material changes, we will notify you through in-app notifications or email.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions, please contact us:

  • Email: yilin01@icloud.com
  • In-App Feedback: Profile > Feedback

12. Compliance

GreenSoul is committed to complying with applicable privacy laws and regulations, including:

  • ✅ General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) - EU
  • ✅ California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) - USA
  • ✅ Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL) - China
  • ✅ Apple App Store Privacy Guidelines
