1.1 What is GreenSoul

GreenSoul is an innovative mobile application that uses AI technology to transform your plants into unique virtual companions, providing emotional support and conversational interaction.

  • Plant Identification: Identify plant species and characteristics using AI
  • Personality Generation: Create unique virtual personalities based on plant traits
